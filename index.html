About Me

I am Gina Bola, your blazing Ball of Sunshine and self-proclaimed Eccentric Multimedia Enthusiast (EME).

Currently, I am taking up BA Multimedia Studies under the Faculty of Information and Communication Studies at the University of the Philippines Open University. From my program and faculty names alone, it's undeniable that I love studying. Fact or Bluff?

“Meron tayong free will, gamitin natin 'to!”

As an unofficially ISO-certified EME (certified by yours truly, effective today as I contemplate about this), I try to incorporate my unconventional, sometimes unhinged humor into every multimedia output I create—as long as I'm allowed.

I also love consuming all sorts of eccentrically chaotic content, especially Philippine Pop Culture. From teleserye drama to iconic memes and OPM, this amalgamation (amalgamation?) has shaped my personality and the way I express myself. Because I believe the children are our future... Mali!

If you're up for eccentric, very random multimedia production like me, I'd be honored to work with you. I'm the first honor, and you're the second. Yahoo!
